# Hi, I'm Yousif

I'm a technical founder and product leader originally from Baghdad, Iraq. I started my career there fixing gaming consoles and selling computer parts before eventually working as a translator for the U.S. Army and transitioning into software development. I came to the United States as a refugee in 2008 and have been building products ever since.

- Built **AppAssure Software**, which was acquired by Dell; led product development post-acquisition
- Recruited by Bain Capital to run **Nubisio** as CEO; secured a $4M Series A term sheet
- VP at **Cloudistics**, later acquired by Fungible (now Microsoft)
- Founded **SocialWeaver**, an employee advocacy platform used by Fortune 200 enterprises; launched as #1 Product of the Day on Product Hunt

These days, I'm focused on building enterprise and SaaS products, creating efficient customer success organizations, and making customer success an integral part of the product itself. I'm also still building things. That part never really stops.

---

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia's School of Engineering and speak both English and Arabic.

I write about what I'm working on and share more about my projects over at [yabood.com](https://yabood.com).
