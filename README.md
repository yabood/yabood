# Hi, I'm Yousif

I grew up in Baghdad, Iraq, and started my early career there fixing gaming consoles and controllers, primarily Sega and later PlayStation. I was always passionate about computers, so I partnered with a friend and opened a small PC shop where we sold computer parts and repaired machines. Think a tiny Micro Center. After the 2003 war started, I took a job as a translator for the U.S. Army before transitioning into software development on a Department of State-funded project, building a database for the Iraqi Justice System. That work opened the door to some of the most impactful projects of my early career: Iraq's first internal database for the Correctional Service and its first internet-based criminal defendant tracking system. Due to my work with the U.S. Army, I was labeled a traitor by local militias and received multiple death threats that forced me to flee. First to Syria, then Jordan, and finally, in 2008, my family and I were admitted as refugees to the United States.

Since arriving in the U.S., I've held technical and leadership roles across startups and large organizations. I helped build AppAssure Software, which was acquired by Dell, where I went on to lead product development. I was recruited by Bain Capital to run Nubisio as CEO, securing a $4 million term sheet during a Series A round. I served as VP at Cloudistics, which was later acquired by Fungible (now Microsoft). Most recently, I founded SocialWeaver, an employee advocacy platform that I took from inception to paying customers, including Fortune 200 enterprises, and launched on Product Hunt as the #1 Product of the Day.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia. I speak English and Arabic.

These days, I'm focused on building enterprise and SaaS products, creating efficient customer success organizations, and making customer success an integral part of the product itself. I'm also still building things. That part never really stops.

I write about what I'm working on and share more about my projects over at [yabood.com](https://yabood.com).
