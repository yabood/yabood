# Hi, I’m Yousif

I’m a technical founder and product leader originally from Baghdad, Iraq. I started out fixing gaming consoles and selling computer parts, worked as a translator for the U.S. Army, and then moved into software development. I came to the United States as a refugee in 2008 and have been building products ever since. [More about me](https://yabood.com/about)

I’m Chief Marketing Officer at Elastio, where I designed and built Sightline, the revenue intelligence platform our team uses daily. I built its MCP server so teammates can ask Claude about deal scores and pipeline health. I’m also building [Lexeme](https://lexeme.com), which helps B2B software teams track how their brands appear in AI-generated answers.

Earlier in my career:

- I led customer success at AppAssure and moved into product development at Dell after the acquisition.
- Bain Capital recruited me to lead Nubisio as CEO, where I secured a $4M Series A term sheet.
- I served as VP of Customer Success at Cloudistics and supported the sale of its assets to Fungible, now part of Microsoft.
- I founded SocialWeaver, an employee advocacy platform used by Fortune 200 enterprises. Our SocialWeaver Content Curation launch ranked [#1 Product of the Day on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/socialweaver).

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia. I’m pursuing a part-time master’s in computer science at Georgia Tech, specializing in machine learning.
