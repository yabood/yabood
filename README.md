# Hi, I'm Yousif

I'm a technical founder and product leader originally from Baghdad, Iraq. I started my career fixing gaming consoles and selling computer parts before working as a translator for the U.S. Army and transitioning into software development. I came to the United States as a refugee in 2008 and have been building products ever since. Learn more: [yabood.com/about](https://yabood.com/about)

- Led customer success at **AppAssure Software**, acquired by Dell; later led product development post-acquisition
- Recruited by Bain Capital to run **Nubisio** as CEO; secured a $4M Series A term sheet
- VP at **Cloudistics**, later acquired by Fungible (now Microsoft)
- Founded **SocialWeaver**, an employee advocacy platform used by Fortune 200 enterprises; launched as #1 Product of the Day on Product Hunt

These days, I focus on building enterprise SaaS products and embedding customer success directly into the product.
